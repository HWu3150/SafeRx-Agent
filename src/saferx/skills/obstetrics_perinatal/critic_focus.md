Default: KEEP. Remove an obstetric code only for a documented block, a missing indication, or a wrong sibling.

- Prior-visit medications are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented.
- New codes need an anchor: pregnancy or postpartum status, cesarean or delivery procedure, a hypertensive, hemorrhagic, infectious, nausea or pain context, or supportive ICU/post-op care.

## Wrong sibling or not documented — remove
- H01BB (oxytocin) unless oxytocin/Pitocin is explicitly named as given; do not infer it from delivery or labor alone.
- G02AB unless atony/PPH refractory to oxytocin is documented.
- D04AB when the only anesthesia is epidural/spinal (N01BB). R02AX without intubation or an airway procedure.
- G01AF for oral candidiasis or bacterial vaginosis; G01AA for candidiasis or when only systemic antibiotics are used.

## Remove if documented
- M01AB: active labor or antepartum, renal impairment, NSAID allergy.
- C02DB: normotensive without an OB hypertensive disorder. G02AB: hypertensive disorder of pregnancy.
- L02AB: routine pregnancy without preterm-birth risk or bleeding indication.
