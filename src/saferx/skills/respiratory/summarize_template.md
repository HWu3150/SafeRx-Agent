Prioritize:
- hypoxia, hypercapnia, respiratory failure, pneumonia, COPD, asthma, aspiration, pleural disease
- intubation, ventilation, tracheostomy, bronchoscopy, oxygen therapy, airway irritation
- ICU context that makes sedation, ulcer prophylaxis, or airway-care classes plausible

When present, capture:
- infection severity, airway edema, secretion burden, and respiratory support trajectory
- overlap with cardiac or fluid-overload causes of dyspnea
