Medication classes are most plausible when supported by:
- bronchodilator, inhaled respiratory, or steroid classes for explicit obstructive airway disease or airway inflammation
- antibacterial classes when pneumonia or documented respiratory infection is explicit
- sedation, analgesia, acid suppression, or airway-symptom relief when ventilation or airway procedures are documented
- diuretic overlap only if the summary clearly shows cardiopulmonary congestion, not purely respiratory infection

Be careful:
- do not add antibiotics for hypoxia alone
- do not add inhaled therapy without actual obstructive or airway evidence

## How to apply
- Predict a class when at least one listed trigger is documented for the current admission, or it is an ongoing prior-visit medication.
- Not enough: a diagnosis name without admission-level context, remote history, or the care setting alone.
- Any **block** → do not predict.
- Within a group the first class is the default; a class marked *second-line* needs its stated condition. One indication → one class unless a combination is noted.

## Obstructive airway disease
- **R03AC** short-acting β2-agonists (albuterol) — asthma or COPD exacerbation; bronchospasm or wheezing on exam; respiratory distress with an obstructive component; home inhaler use. **Block:** respiratory failure or pneumonia without obstruction or wheeze; desaturation alone.
- **R03BA** inhaled corticosteroids (budesonide, fluticasone) — persistent asthma (GINA controller); COPD with ≥2 exacerbations/year or ≥1 hospitalization (GOLD); eosinophilic airway disease; home ICS; ventilated patient with asthma/reactive airway disease. Systemic steroids are H02AB; nasal steroids are not R03BA. **Block:** pneumonia without asthma/COPD; stable COPD without exacerbation history; one-off wheeze; systemic steroid use alone.

## Respiratory infection
- **J01DE** cefepime — HAP or VAP; pneumonia with respiratory failure needing ICU coverage; respiratory-source sepsis needing antipseudomonal coverage; MDR risk (prior IV antibiotics, ventilation ≥5 days, structural lung disease, prior Pseudomonas). **Block:** ambulatory CAP without MDR risk; viral pneumonia without superinfection; COPD exacerbation without pneumonia; MRSA coverage only; documented de-escalation.

## Ventilation and sedation
- **N01AX** propofol/ketamine — prolonged MV with IV sedation protocol; agitation on the ventilator; tracheostomy needing sedation or procedural analgesia; bronchoscopy or airway intervention. **Block:** extubated or spontaneously breathing; CPAP/BiPAP without procedural sedation; mild hypoxia without intubation.

## Cough and secretions
- **R05CA** expectorants (guaifenesin) — productive cough with thick secretions; bronchitis or lower respiratory infection with mucus hypersecretion; guaifenesin ordered. **Block:** dry cough (that is R05DB); intubated with adequate suctioning.
- **R05DB** cough suppressants (dextromethorphan, benzonatate) — dry or non-productive cough disrupting sleep; post-infectious cough; suppressant ordered. **Block:** productive cough or pneumonia with clearance needs; intubated; hemoptysis.

## Allergy
- **R06AX** diphenhydramine and others — allergic reaction or anaphylaxis adjunct; diphenhydramine documented for allergy, pruritus or insomnia; pre-medication before blood products or infusions. **Block:** no allergic indication; sedation in a ventilated patient.
- **R06AE** cetirizine/hydroxyzine — allergic rhinitis; urticaria or pruritus; hydroxyzine for anxiety or pruritus. **Block:** no allergic component; sedation alone.

## Cardiopulmonary overlap
- **C03CA** loop diuretics — pulmonary edema or congestion with respiratory failure; ARDS or respiratory failure with volume overload; cardiac pleural effusion. **Block:** pneumonia, infection or COPD exacerbation without a volume component.
- **C01BB** lidocaine (antiarrhythmic) — only documented VT/VF, post-arrest care, or ACLS for ventricular ectopy. Topical or analgesic lidocaine is N01BB or D04AB. **Block:** ventilator analgesia; intubation without ventricular arrhythmia.

## Prevention
- **J07BB** influenza vaccine — vaccination ordered or discussed; influenza-season admission with vaccine consideration in the care plan. **Block:** no vaccination mention; immunocompromised where a live vaccine is contraindicated.
