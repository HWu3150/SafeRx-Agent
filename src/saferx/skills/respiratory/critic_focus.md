Default: KEEP. Remove a respiratory code only for a documented block, a missing anchor, or a wrong sibling.

- Prior-visit respiratory medications (including home inhalers) are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented.
- New codes need an anchor: explicit lung, airway or infectious disease; an airway procedure or ventilation; symptom relief tied to respiratory support. Inhaled therapy needs documented obstruction or airway inflammation, not ventilation alone.

## Wrong sibling or missing anchor — remove
- R05CA with dry cough; R05DB with productive cough or clearance needs.
- R06AX or R06AE used only for sedation without an allergic indication.
- C01BB without documented ventricular arrhythmia (lidocaine for pain is N01BB).
- C03CA for pneumonia or infection without volume overload.
- R03BA for stable COPD without exacerbation history, or for pneumonia without asthma/COPD.

## Remove if documented
- J01DE: CAP without MDR risk, or de-escalation to narrower coverage.
- N01AX: extubated, spontaneously breathing, or no sedation need.
- R03AC: no obstructive disease or bronchospasm.
- R05CA: intubated with adequate suctioning. R05DB: intubated or hemoptysis.
