Medication classes are most plausible when supported by:
- analgesics for fracture, trauma, severe post-op pain, or painful wound care
- anticoagulant prophylaxis when surgery, immobility, or trauma raises VTE risk
- antiemetic and bowel-regimen classes when opioid or post-op context is explicit
- antibiotics only when open wound, surgical infection risk, or documented infection is present
- mineral/electrolyte support only when repletion or losses are actually suggested

Be careful:
- do not predict broad trauma bundles without evidence
- keep the distinction between orthopedic support and unrelated chronic disease meds

## How to apply
- Predict a class when at least one listed trigger is documented for the current admission, or it is an ongoing prior-visit medication.
- Not enough: a diagnosis name without admission-level context, remote history, or the care setting alone.
- Any **block** → do not predict.
- Within a group the first class is the default; a class marked *second-line* needs its stated condition. One indication → one class unless a combination is noted.

## Analgesia (match the class to pain severity)
- **N02AA** strong opioids — major joint replacement; spine surgery/fusion; polytrauma or multiple fractures; burns; rib fractures with pain needing strong opioids; analgesia in a ventilated trauma patient. **Block:** cast/splint or minor procedures; consultation without surgery; chronic stable pain without an acute cause; opioid-naïve with mild injury; opioid allergy.
- **N02AX** tramadol — moderate pain stepping up from non-opioids; step-down from N02AA; moderate fracture pain where strong opioids are not warranted; elderly patients. **Block:** severe pain; seizure history; SSRI/SNRI co-use without serotonin-syndrome awareness; mild pain manageable with NSAIDs.
- **N02AJ** opioid combinations (codeine + paracetamol) — mild-moderate pain; step-down analgesia. **Block:** severe pain; CYP2D6 poor metabolizer; respiratory-depression risk.
- **M01AB** systemic NSAIDs (ketorolac, diclofenac) — post-op or fracture pain as opioid-sparing adjunct; MSK inflammation (tendinitis, bursitis, degenerative flare); ketorolac IV/IM ≤5 days; renal colic or acute gout. **Block (all systemic NSAIDs):** AKI, CKD ≥3 or GFR <60; active GI bleed or ulcer history; therapeutic anticoagulation; post-CABG; severe HF; thrombocytopenia.
- **M01AE** ibuprofen, *second-line* — same indications as M01AB when ketorolac/diclofenac is not chosen; same blocks.
- **M02AA** topical NSAIDs — localized joint/tendon/soft-tissue pain or OA flare, especially when systemic NSAIDs are blocked. **Block:** deep injury or widespread pain needing systemic analgesia; open wound at the site.
- **N01BB** local anesthetics — peripheral nerve block (femoral, interscalene, popliteal); regional/neuraxial anesthesia; lidocaine patch; wound infiltration. Systemic lidocaine for arrhythmia is C01BB. **Block:** general anesthesia without a regional component.

## Neuropathic pain and chronic adjuncts
- **N03AX** gabapentinoids — home gabapentin/pregabalin; radiculopathy or neurogenic claudication; spine surgery with nerve-root involvement; neuropathic pain (diabetic, post-herpetic); epilepsy on therapy. **Block:** fracture or sprain without nerve involvement; spine surgery without neuropathic findings; elderly with respiratory disease or opioid co-use and no prior gabapentinoid.
- **N06AX** SNRIs/TCAs/mirtazapine — prior antidepressant for MDD/anxiety; duloxetine or venlafaxine for neuropathic/MSK pain; chronic pain with depression; mirtazapine for sleep/appetite. **Block:** acute trauma without psychiatric history; new low mood after injury without a chronic diagnosis.
- **N03AE** clonazepam — seizure disorder on clonazepam; neuropathic pain adjunct in SCI or CRPS; spasticity when baclofen/tizanidine is insufficient; myoclonus. **Block:** anxiety or insomnia alone; acute sedation.

## Muscle relaxation and paralysis
- **M03BA** methocarbamol — acute muscle spasm from trauma, strain or back injury. **Block:** chronic spasticity (that is M03BX); no documented spasm.
- **M03BX** baclofen/tizanidine — chronic spasticity (SCI, MS, cerebral palsy); baclofen pump or home baclofen/tizanidine; severe spasm not responding to M03BA. **Block:** simple strain without spasticity; neuromuscular-blockade context (that is M03AC).
- **M03AC** neuromuscular blockers — RSI/emergency airway; MV needing paralysis (ARDS, dyssynchrony); surgery requiring paralysis. Not a muscle relaxant. **Block:** muscle spasm; non-intubated without surgery.

## Supportive and other
- **A06AB** stimulant laxatives — opioid use this admission; post-op bowel regimen; immobility with constipation; neurogenic bowel after spinal injury. **Block:** diarrhea, ileus, bowel obstruction.
- **C01BB** lidocaine (antiarrhythmic) — only documented VT/VF, ACLS during trauma resuscitation, or post-arrest care. **Block:** pain or procedure context (that is N01BB).
