Default: KEEP. Remove a musculoskeletal/trauma code only for a documented block, a missing indication, or a wrong sibling.

- Prior-visit medications are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented.
- New codes need an anchor: painful injury or procedure, immobility or post-op VTE risk, wound or hardware infection, or a post-op/rehabilitation setting.

## Wrong sibling — remove
- C01BB without documented ventricular arrhythmia (lidocaine for pain or blocks is N01BB).
- M03AC for muscle spasm (it is a neuromuscular blocker; keep only for intubation, paralysis or surgery).
- M03BX for simple acute spasm (M03BA), or M03BA for chronic spasticity (M03BX).
- Opioid class mismatched to severity: N02AX or N02AJ when pain clearly needs strong opioids; N02AA for mild injury only.
- N03AE or N06AX for anxiety/insomnia alone without a neurologic, pain or psychiatric indication.

## Remove if documented
- Systemic NSAIDs (M01AB, M01AE): AKI, CKD ≥3, active GI bleed, therapeutic anticoagulation, post-CABG.
- M02AA: no localized pain target or open wound at the site.
- N02AA: no surgical/trauma pain, opioid allergy or intolerance. N02AX: seizure history without explicit tramadol continuation. N02AJ: CYP2D6 poor metabolizer.
- N03AX: spine surgery without neuropathic findings and no home gabapentinoid.
- A06AB: diarrhea or ileus.
