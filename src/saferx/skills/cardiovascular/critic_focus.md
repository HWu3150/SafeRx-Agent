Default: KEEP. Remove a cardiovascular code only for a documented block, a missing indication, or a duplicate.

- Prior-visit cardiovascular medications are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented. Do not remove chronic drugs because the history is old.
- New codes need a concrete indication: AF/flutter, CAD/MI, HF, cardiomyopathy, valve or vascular disease, post-procedure state, fluid overload, BP control, or rhythm instability.

## Combinations that are standard — do not remove for overlap
- Aspirin (N02BA) + P2Y12 (B01AC) after ACS/stent.
- Heparin (B01AB) overlapping an oral anticoagulant during bridging.

## Duplicates and wrong siblings — remove
- ACEi (C09AA) + ARB (C09CA) together → keep one.
- Warfarin (B01AA) when a DOAC (B01AF) covers the same indication; two anticoagulants without bridging.
- C01BB when lidocaine is for pain or local anesthesia (that is N01BB) or no ventricular arrhythmia is documented.
- C07AA or C08CA proposed for AF rate control (C07AB / C08DB are the rate-control classes).
- C03BA without a loop diuretic.

## Remove if documented
- β-blockers (C07AB, C07AA, C07AG): cardiogenic shock, HR <50, high-degree AV block, ADHF needing inotropes, severe bronchospasm.
- C08DB: HFrEF, hypotension. C08CA: acute decompensated HF, cardiogenic shock.
- C09AA / C09CA: K⁺ >5.5, AKI with rising creatinine, ACEi angioedema/cough (C09AA), pregnancy (C09CA).
- B01AB: HIT, active major bleeding, platelets <50k without thrombotic emergency. B01AF: CrCl <15, mechanical valve, major bleeding. B01AA: INR critically high without reversal plan, major bleeding. B01AC / N02BA: active major bleeding, severe thrombocytopenia, aspirin allergy.
- C01DA: SBP <90, RV infarction, PDE5 inhibitor, severe aortic stenosis. C01CA: hemodynamically stable without shock or inotrope need.
- C01AA: severe hypokalemia, high-degree AV block, VT. C01BD: severe QT prolongation, no arrhythmia indication.
- C03CA: hypovolemia or hypotension without overload. C03AA: severe hyponatremia, gout flare, GFR <30.
- C02DB: aortic dissection without β-blocker, hypotension. C02AC: severe bradycardia, instability.
- C10AA / C10AX: active hepatic failure, rhabdomyolysis (C10AA), no lipid or CV disease.
