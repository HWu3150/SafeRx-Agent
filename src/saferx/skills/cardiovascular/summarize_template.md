Prioritize:
- rhythm problems, ischemic symptoms, decompensated heart failure, edema, chest pain, cardiomyopathy
- blood pressure context, perfusion issues, diuresis needs, anticoagulation history
- cardiac procedures or devices such as stents, CABG, valves, pacers, vascular interventions

When present, capture:
- evidence for rate control, antithrombotic continuation, diuresis, vasodilator use
- cardiorenal interaction and respiratory consequences of fluid overload
