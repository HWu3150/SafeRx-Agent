Medication classes are most plausible when the summary supports one or more of:
- antiplatelet or anticoagulant therapy tied to AF, ACS, VTE history, valve or vascular context
- beta-blocker, rate/rhythm control, or antihypertensive classes tied to arrhythmia, HF, CAD, severe hypertension
- diuretics for congestion or volume overload
- antianginal, vasodilator, lipid-lowering, or heart-failure-directed classes only when there is explicit chronic or acute evidence

## How to apply
- Predict a class when at least one listed trigger is documented for the current admission, or it is an ongoing prior-visit medication.
- Not enough: a diagnosis name without admission-level context, remote history, or the care setting alone.
- Any **block** → do not predict.
- Within a group the first class is the default; a class marked *second-line* needs its stated condition. One indication → one class, except standard combinations noted below.

## Antithrombotics
Standard combinations: aspirin + P2Y12 (DAPT) after ACS/stent; heparin overlapping an oral anticoagulant while bridging.
- **B01AB** heparin — ACS; DVT/PE treatment or bridging; AF starting or bridging anticoagulation; post-PCI or cardiac surgery; mechanical valve bridging; acute limb ischemia/arterial thrombosis; VTE prophylaxis in a critically ill cardiovascular patient. **Block:** stable AF already on an oral anticoagulant without bridging need; active major bleeding (intracranial, GI); HIT; platelets <50k without a thrombotic emergency.
- **N02BA** aspirin — CAD/ACS/NSTEMI/STEMI; prior stent, CABG or PCI; PAD; ischemic stroke/TIA; antiplatelet documented (e.g., Z7982). **Block:** AF without CAD when fully anticoagulated; active GI bleeding; aspirin allergy; thrombocytopenia.
- **B01AC** P2Y12 inhibitors (clopidogrel, ticagrelor) — ACS; post-PCI/stent; recent CABG or peripheral intervention. **Block:** AF alone; stable CAD on aspirin without stent/ACS; active bleeding or high bleeding risk without clear indication.
- **B01AF** factor Xa inhibitors — AF needing oral anticoagulation (preferred over warfarin, CrCl ≥25–30); DVT/PE treatment or prophylaxis with adequate renal function. **Block:** CrCl <15; mechanical valve; active major bleeding; another anticoagulant already given.
- **B01AA** warfarin, *second-line* — mechanical valve; AF with documented warfarin use; DVT/PE when a DOAC is contraindicated. **Block:** DOAC already prescribed; no warfarin history; active bleeding; INR supratherapeutic.

## Rate and rhythm control
- **C07AB** selective β-blockers — AF/flutter rate control; ACS; HFrEF (EF <40%) or cardiomyopathy; post-MI or cardiac surgery. **Block:** HTN alone without a cardiac diagnosis; cardiogenic shock; HR <50 or high-degree AV block; acute decompensated HF needing inotropes; severe bronchospasm.
- **C08DB** diltiazem, *second-line* — AF/flutter rate control or angina when β-blockers are contraindicated or insufficient. **Block:** HFrEF; β-blocker with AV-block risk; hypotension; wide-complex tachycardia of uncertain origin.
- **C01AA** digoxin, *second-line* — AF with rapid rate refractory to β-blocker/CCB; HFrEF symptomatic despite guideline therapy. **Block:** sinus rhythm without HF; hypokalemia or renal failure without dose plan; AV block; VT.
- **C01BD** amiodarone, *second-line* — AF rate/rhythm control when first-line agents fail; refractory VT/VF (ACLS); post-arrest loading. **Block:** stable sinus rhythm without arrhythmia history; thyroid disease without arrhythmia; QT prolongation without clear benefit.
- **C01BB** lidocaine (antiarrhythmic) — VT/VF, electrical storm, post-arrest with shockable rhythm, post-defibrillation suppression, lidocaine infusion for arrhythmia. Lidocaine for pain or local anesthesia is **N01BB**, not C01BB. **Block:** atrial arrhythmias, SVT, stable bradycardia.

## Heart failure, congestion and blood pressure
- **C03CA** loop diuretics — acute decompensated HF; volume overload, pulmonary or peripheral edema; valve disease with fluid retention; CKD/nephrotic syndrome with overload; ICU fluid overload. **Block:** HTN or stable CKD without overload; hypovolemia/dehydration.
- **C03BA** metolazone, *second-line* — diuretic resistance as adjunct to a loop diuretic. **Block:** first-line diuresis; isolated HTN; severe hyponatremia or hypokalemia.
- **C09AA** ACE inhibitors — HFrEF or post-MI remodeling; diabetic nephropathy or proteinuric CKD; HTN with prior ACEi. **Block:** hyperkalemia (K⁺ >5.5); bilateral renal artery stenosis; AKI with rising creatinine; ACEi cough or angioedema.
- **C09CA** ARBs, *second-line* — HTN, HFpEF, HFrEF or diabetic nephropathy when ACEi is not tolerated. **Block:** already on an ACEi (never combine); hyperkalemia; bilateral renal artery stenosis; AKI with rising creatinine; pregnancy.
- **C08CA** dihydropyridine CCBs — HTN without HFrEF; vasospastic or stable angina without systolic dysfunction. **Block:** acute decompensated HF; cardiogenic shock; AF rate control (that is C08DA/C08DB).
- **C03AA** thiazides — HTN (first-line or added for resistant HTN); chronic edema not needing a loop diuretic. **Block:** acute decompensated HF; severe hyponatremia; gout; GFR <30.
- **C07AA** non-selective β-blockers — portal hypertension/variceal prophylaxis; HFrEF (carvedilol); tremor with documented use. **Block:** AF rate control (use C07AB); asthma/severe COPD; decompensated cirrhosis with refractory ascites and hypotension; cardiogenic shock.
- **C07AG** labetalol — hypertensive emergency/urgency; aortic dissection; perioperative HTN; preeclampsia/eclampsia. **Block:** stable chronic HTN; bradycardia/AV block; cardiogenic shock; severe bronchospasm.
- **C02DB** hydralazine — severe HTN/hypertensive emergency needing IV vasodilator; preeclampsia/eclampsia; HFrEF with isosorbide when ACEi/ARB intolerant. **Block:** mild-moderate HTN; aortic dissection without β-blocker; CAD without HF (reflex tachycardia).
- **C02AC** clonidine, *second-line* — resistant HTN adjunct; hypertensive urgency; opioid/alcohol withdrawal with sympathetic surge; perioperative HTN. **Block:** first-line HTN; severe bradycardia; hemodynamic instability.

## Ischemia, shock and lipids
- **C01DA** nitrates — ACS chest pain; hypertensive emergency with ischemia; stable or vasospastic angina; pulmonary edema with high BP. **Block:** SBP <90; PDE5 inhibitor use; RV infarction; severe aortic stenosis; hypovolemic shock.
- **C01CA** vasopressors/inotropes — cardiogenic shock; post-arrest support; ADHF with hypotension; septic shock with cardiac dysfunction; post-cardiac-surgery instability; documented vasopressor need. **Block:** stable HF without hypotension; HTN; isolated bradycardia; ward-level care without shock.
- **C10AA** statins — hyperlipidemia (E78xx); ASCVD (CAD, stroke, PAD); T2DM age ≥40 with CV risk. **Block:** acute illness without lipid history; active hepatic failure; rhabdomyolysis.
- **C10AX** ezetimibe, *second-line* — statin intolerance; add-on when LDL not at target. **Block:** no lipid or CV disease; active hepatic disease.
