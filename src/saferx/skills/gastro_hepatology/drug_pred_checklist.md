Medication classes are most plausible when supported by:
- acid suppression for GI bleed, ulcer, severe reflux, or feeding-tube/post-op context
- bowel regimen classes when immobility, opioids, ileus risk, or constipation is explicit
- antiemetics for nausea-prone post-op or GI disease context
- lactulose or hepatic-supportive classes when cirrhosis/hepatic encephalopathy is explicit
- nutrition-related classes when enteral feeding or malnutrition support is documented
- antibiotics only with infection or procedural contamination context

## How to apply
- Predict a class when at least one listed trigger is documented for the current admission, or it is an ongoing prior-visit medication.
- Not enough: a diagnosis name without admission-level context, remote history, or the care setting alone.
- Any **block** → do not predict.
- Within a group the first class is the default; a class marked *second-line* needs its stated condition. One indication → one class unless a combination is noted.

## Cirrhosis and portal hypertension
Standard combination: spironolactone + loop diuretic for ascites.
- **C03DA** spironolactone — cirrhosis **with** ascites (K703x, K7469, R18x); portal hypertension with fluid retention on diuretics; hepatic decompensation needing diuresis. **Block:** cirrhosis without ascites; hepatitis or elevated LFTs alone; K⁺ >5.5; AKI with rising creatinine; ESRD on dialysis.
- **C03CA** loop diuretics — cirrhosis with ascites needing diuresis; portal hypertension with sodium retention/overload; hepatorenal syndrome management. **Block:** no ascites or overload; hypovolemia; hyponatremia without overload.
- **A12CC** magnesium — alcohol-related liver disease; cirrhosis on a loop diuretic; severe malnutrition with electrolyte monitoring; hypomagnesemia or Mg replacement noted. **Block:** liver disease without alcohol, diuretic or Mg context; hypermagnesemia.

## Intestinal anti-infectives
- **A07AA** intestinal antibiotics — hepatic encephalopathy (rifaximin with lactulose; K7290, K742x); oral candidiasis (nystatin; B370); C. difficile (oral vancomycin, fidaxomicin); cirrhosis with documented oral SBP prophylaxis. Systemic infections use J01 codes. **Block:** cirrhosis without HE, candidiasis or C. diff.

## Hepatobiliary diagnostics
- **V04CX** diagnostic agents (secretin, hepatobiliary tests) — cholestasis or biliary obstruction workup; ERCP or hepatobiliary procedure needing a diagnostic agent; pancreatobiliary assessment. **Block:** cirrhosis or liver disease without a diagnostic procedure.
