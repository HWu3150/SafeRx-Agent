Prioritize:
- GI bleeding, ulcer disease, cirrhosis, portal hypertension, hepatic encephalopathy, pancreatitis, cholangitis
- nausea/vomiting, bowel obstruction, constipation/ileus, diarrhea, enteral feeding
- endoscopy, abdominal surgery, drains, stomas, NG/PEG/feeding tubes

When present, capture:
- hepatic synthetic dysfunction, ascites, ammonia-lowering context
- procedure-linked prophylaxis or antiemetic needs
