Default: KEEP. Remove a gastro/hepatology code only for a documented block, a missing indication, or a duplicate.

- Prior-visit medications are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented.
- New codes need an anchor: acid suppression → GI bleed, ulcer, severe reflux, feeding tube or post-op; hepatic classes → cirrhosis, encephalopathy or decompensation; bowel regimen → opioids, immobility, post-op or constipation.
- Spironolactone + loop diuretic for ascites is a standard combination, not a duplicate.

## Remove if documented
- C03DA: no ascites, K⁺ >5.5, AKI with rising creatinine, ESRD on dialysis.
- C03CA: hypovolemia, or hyponatremia without overload.
- A12CC: hypermagnesemia, renal failure without a replacement plan.
- A07AA: only a systemic infection without HE, candidiasis or C. diff (systemic antibiotics are J01).
