Default: KEEP. Remove an oncology/hematology code only for a documented block, a missing anchor, or a wrong sibling.

- Prior-visit medications are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented.
- New codes need an anchor: active cancer treatment or symptom burden; cytopenia, thrombosis, bleeding or infection risk; an explicit treatment or supportive-care plan. Keep supportive oncology classes (antiemetics, growth factors, anticoagulants) when their own anchor is documented.

## Missing anchor — remove
- L01AA, L01CB, L01DB, L01XC: no active regimen containing the agent; diagnosis or tumor type alone is not enough.
- L03AA: no myelosuppression or neutropenia. B03XA: no chemotherapy or CKD context, or acute blood loss.
- J01DD / J01DB: no fever, neutropenia or documented infection source.
- A07AA: systemic infection without an oral, intestinal or HE indication (use J01 codes).
- H02AB: solid tumor without cerebral, lymphomatous or steroid-responsive indication and no prior steroid.

## Remove if documented
- B01AC: platelets <50k. L01DB: cumulative dose limit or anthracycline cardiotoxicity.
- B02BA: therapeutic INR without reversal need; thrombocytopenia without a coagulation-factor problem.
- B02BD: no factor deficiency; DIC managed with FFP/cryoprecipitate only.
