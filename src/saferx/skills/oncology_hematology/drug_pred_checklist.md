Medication classes are most plausible when supported by:
- anticoagulation or antiplatelet classes when clotting risk is explicit and bleeding context does not contradict it
- analgesic, antiemetic, acid suppression, bowel-regimen, or appetite/supportive classes when cancer care or symptom burden supports them
- anti-infective classes when neutropenia, fever, line infection risk, or documented infection is explicit
- hematologic support only when anemia, thrombocytopenia, bleeding, or transfusion context is present

Be careful:
- preserve the tension between thrombosis prevention and bleeding risk

## How to apply
- Predict a class when at least one listed trigger is documented for the current admission, or it is an ongoing prior-visit medication.
- Not enough: a diagnosis name without admission-level context, remote history, or the care setting alone.
- Any **block** → do not predict.
- Within a group the first class is the default; a class marked *second-line* needs its stated condition. One indication → one class unless a combination is noted.

## Chemotherapy and targeted therapy
Predict an antineoplastic class only when an active regimen containing that agent is documented (administered this admission or in the current treatment plan). A cancer diagnosis, tumor type, or completed chemotherapy is not enough.
- **L01AA** cyclophosphamide — CHOP, R-CHOP, CVP or another regimen containing it; lupus nephritis or vasculitis treated by hematology.
- **L01DB** doxorubicin — CHOP, R-CHOP, AC, ABVD; breast cancer, lymphoma or sarcoma on an anthracycline regimen. **Block:** cumulative dose limit reached; documented anthracycline cardiotoxicity.
- **L01CB** etoposide — EP, BEP, ICE; small-cell lung cancer, germ-cell tumor or lymphoma on an etoposide regimen.
- **L01XC** rituximab/monoclonal antibodies — B-cell NHL on R-CHOP, R-CVP or R-EPOCH; CLL or other hematologic malignancy on antibody therapy; anti-CD20 targeted protocol. **Block:** solid tumor without antibody therapy; autoimmune use outside oncology/hematology.

## Steroids
- **H02AB** glucocorticoids — brain metastasis or cerebral edema (C79x, G936); head/neck malignancy with airway edema; lymphoma/leukemia with steroid in the regimen or for complications; immunotherapy/chemotherapy hypersensitivity or inflammatory toxicity. **Block:** solid tumor without a steroid-responsive indication; antiemetic use alone; SIADH without cerebral edema or adrenal insufficiency.

## Infection in the immunocompromised
- **J01DD / J01DB** cephalosporins — neutropenic fever; line infection or bacteremia risk in an immunocompromised patient; documented gram-negative infection. **Block:** cancer without fever, neutropenia or infection source.
- **A07AA** intestinal anti-infectives (nystatin, rifaximin, oral vancomycin) — oral candidiasis or stomatitis (B370); mucositis with fungal superinfection; hepatic encephalopathy; C. difficile. Systemic infections use J01 codes. **Block:** no oral, intestinal or HE indication.

## Cytopenias and hematologic support
- **L03AA** G-CSF (filgrastim, pegfilgrastim) — neutropenia after myelosuppressive chemotherapy (primary or secondary prophylaxis); febrile neutropenia; stem-cell mobilization; G-CSF in the supportive-care plan. **Block:** stable counts without myelosuppression; infection without neutropenia.
- **B03XA** ESAs (epoetin, darbepoetin) — chemotherapy-induced anemia with Hb <10 g/dL; CKD anemia; documented ESA use. **Block:** acute blood loss (transfuse); iron-deficiency anemia; Hb >10 without symptoms.

## Coagulation and antithrombotics
- **B02BA** vitamin K — warfarin reversal (supratherapeutic INR or bleeding on warfarin); coagulopathy with high INR from liver disease or malnutrition; pre-procedural INR correction; neonatal prophylaxis. **Block:** stable therapeutic INR; thrombocytopenia alone; DIC without factor depletion.
- **B02BD** coagulation factors (VIII, IX) — hemophilia A/B or another documented factor deficiency, for bleeding or prophylaxis. **Block:** thrombocytopenia without factor deficiency; DIC managed with FFP/cryoprecipitate; cancer-associated VTE.
- **B01AC** antiplatelets — cancer patient with CAD, stent or another cardiac antiplatelet indication. **Block:** platelets <50k; cancer alone without a thrombotic or cardiac indication.
