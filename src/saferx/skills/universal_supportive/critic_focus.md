Review lens: universal supportive care. Keep a code when the record meets its evidence bar; remove it when the only support is the care setting.

## How to judge
- `[context]` classes — keep with any patient-specific signal relevant to the class; when in doubt, keep: **B01AB, A12BA, A12AA, A04AA, V06DC**.
- `[specific]` classes — keep only if one of the triggers below is documented; when in doubt, remove. All other universal codes are `[specific]`.
- Remove if the only support is ICU admission, hospitalization, NPO, IV access, intubation, general critical illness, immobility, or opioid use alone (opioid use does support A06AD).
- One signal → one class: if two codes rest on the same single signal, keep the default (A06AD over A06AA/A06AB; A02BC over A02BA/A02BX; A04AA over A04AD/A03FA).

## Keep only if (specific classes)
- N02BE: pain, fever, post-op/procedure, multimodal analgesia, or named. N02AA: major surgery, severe trauma/burns, MV with analgesia protocol, or named.
- N02BA: antiplatelet indication (CAD/stent/ACS/MI, stroke/TIA, PAD) or named — not for pain.
- A02BC: PPI at home, GI bleed/ulcer/GERD, or MV **and** (coagulopathy/shock/vasopressors/liver disease). A02BA: H2 named or PPI intolerance. A02BX: sucralfate named or PPI/H2 contraindicated.
- A06AD: opioid use, constipation/bowel regimen, lactulose for HE, post-op immobility. A06AB: bowel regimen or constipation documented with opioids/immobility, or named. A06AA: docusate named. A06AH: documented laxative failure or named.
- A12CC: low Mg, Mg repletion, QT/torsades, alcohol withdrawal, or diuretic Mg loss. B05XA: named crystalloid, resuscitation, or IV electrolyte correction. B05AA: albumin ordered, SBP, large-volume paracentesis, or HRS.
- N01AX: intubated with sedation need or named. N05CH: documented ICU sleep disruption/delirium bundle or named. J07BB: adult, Oct–Apr, no contraindication, or ordered.
- A04AD, A03FA, A11CC, A11DA, B03BA, D04AB, R02AA, R02AD, R02AX, J01MA: the drug is named or its specific indication/procedure is documented.

## Remove regardless of evidence
- B01AB: active bleeding, HIT, anticoagulation contraindication. A12BA: hyperkalemia.
- Any laxative: diarrhea, ileus, bowel obstruction.
- J07BB: severe egg allergy, Guillain-Barré within 6 weeks.
