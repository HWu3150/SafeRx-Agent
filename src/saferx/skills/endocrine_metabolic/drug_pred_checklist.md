Medication classes are most plausible when supported by:
- glucose-lowering or glucose-rescue agents for diabetes or documented dysglycemia
- electrolyte/mineral classes when depletion, dialysis, diuresis, or repletion is explicit
- phosphate binders, vitamin D analogs, or calcium-regulation classes when ESRD/metabolic bone disease is explicit
- thyroid or steroid-related endocrine classes only when the record names the disorder or the medication

Be careful:
- do not infer chronic oral diabetes drugs without medication or continuation evidence
- separate metabolic support from unrelated organ-system therapy

## How to apply
- Predict a class when at least one listed trigger is documented for the current admission, or it is an ongoing prior-visit medication.
- Not enough: a diagnosis name without admission-level context, remote history, or the care setting alone.
- Any **block** → do not predict.
- Within a group the first class is the default; a class marked *second-line* needs its stated condition. One indication → one class unless a combination is noted.

## Diabetes and glucose
- **A10BA** metformin — T2DM with metformin continuation; new T2DM with first-line oral therapy planned; PCOS with insulin resistance; documented metformin plan for pre-diabetes. **Block:** GFR <30; AKI; lactic-acidosis risk (sepsis, shock, hepatic failure); contrast within 48 h; decompensated HF with hypoperfusion.
- **A10BB** sulfonylureas, *second-line* — T2DM needing an added oral agent, with sulfonylurea at home. **Block:** hypoglycemia risk (elderly, malnourished, irregular meals) or recurrent hypoglycemia; renal failure; hepatic impairment; acute illness where oral agents are held.
- **H04AA** glucagon — severe hypoglycemia (<54 mg/dL or altered consciousness) or insulin-treated patient unable to take oral glucose; β-blocker or CCB overdose; anaphylaxis refractory to epinephrine in a β-blocked patient. **Block:** mild hypoglycemia; routine diabetes care.

## Thyroid, adrenal and parathyroid
- **H03AA** levothyroxine — hypothyroidism (E039, E890, E030); thyroidectomy; thyroid cancer on TSH suppression; myxedema. **Block:** TSH monitoring or obesity without hypothyroidism.
- **H03BB** methimazole/PTU — hyperthyroidism (Graves, toxic goiter); thyroid storm; pre-thyroidectomy preparation. **Block:** hypothyroidism; euthyroid sick syndrome; nodule without hyperthyroidism.
- **H02AB** glucocorticoids (endocrine use) — adrenal insufficiency or crisis (E271–E273); long-term steroid continuation; Cushing treatment or steroid taper. Oncologic or inflammatory steroid use belongs to other experts.
- **H02AA** fludrocortisone — primary adrenal insufficiency (Addison); autonomic orthostatic hypotension. **Block:** secondary adrenal insufficiency; HTN.
- **H05BX** cinacalcet — secondary hyperparathyroidism in CKD/dialysis; parathyroid carcinoma with hypercalcemia; primary hyperparathyroidism unfit for surgery. **Block:** hypocalcemia; no PTH or calcium disorder.

## Electrolytes and renal-metabolic
- **V03AE** potassium/phosphate binders — hyperkalemia (K⁺ >5.5) needing a binder; hyperphosphatemia in CKD/ESRD; dialysis phosphate-binder regimen. **Block:** normal K⁺/phosphate; hypokalemia; no renal context for binders.

## Comorbidity classes (when cardiometabolic context is present)
- **C10AA** statins — hyperlipidemia (E78xx); T2DM age ≥40 with CV risk; ASCVD. **Block:** DKA/HHS alone without lipid history; active hepatic failure; rhabdomyolysis.
- **C08CA** dihydropyridine CCBs — HTN with diabetes/metabolic syndrome; severe HTN needing a CCB. **Block:** acute decompensated HFrEF; cardiogenic shock; AF rate control (that is C08DA/C08DB).
- **A06AB** stimulant laxatives — opioid use this admission; post-op bowel management; diabetic gastroparesis or constipation; immobility with bowel management. **Block:** diarrhea, ileus, bowel obstruction.
