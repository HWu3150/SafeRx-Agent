Default: KEEP. Remove an endocrine/metabolic code only for a documented block, a missing indication, or a duplicate.

- Prior-visit endocrine and metabolic medications are kept unless discontinuation or a block is documented; ICU patients routinely continue them.
- New codes need an anchor: diabetes or dysglycemia, an electrolyte/acid-base/mineral disorder, dialysis-linked management, or an explicit thyroid/adrenal/parathyroid diagnosis.

## Wrong direction or wrong owner — remove
- H03BB with hypothyroidism; H05BX with hypocalcemia; V03AE with hypokalemia or normal levels.
- H02AB proposed here only for an oncologic or inflammatory indication (other experts own those).
- H04AA for routine diabetes care without a hypoglycemic episode or overdose.

## Remove if documented
- A10BA: GFR <30, AKI, lactic-acidosis risk, contrast within 48 h.
- A10BB: documented hypoglycemia, renal failure, acute illness where oral agents are held.
- C08CA: acute decompensated HFrEF. C10AA: active hepatic failure, rhabdomyolysis.
- H03AA, H02AA, H05BX: no corresponding diagnosis and no prior use.
